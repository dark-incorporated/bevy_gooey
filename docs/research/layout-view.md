# What was left of the editor's hand-built nodes after G-89

Researched 2026-11-14 for issue #101 (G-90), asked by bevy_dark_factory (the Cauldron), #260 (UI-99), tracking #208. After the editor adopted G-84 to G-89 its raw `Node` literals and hand patches went from 230 to 80; what was left that gooey can solve is here. Each item is a general need; the editor is the example and its name is in no API.

## Method

The rules of G-84 (`docs/research/layout-gaps.md`) stand: a layout choice is data on the node, never a literal in the host and never a number in `apply_skin`; the constructor or component puts it on the node and `apply_skin` writes the `Node`, so it follows the skin and the player's text size. G-84's review added one: **every field is an `Option`, and a field left `None` leaves the node alone** (`RowLayout`), so a component sets only what it names and never resets what a role or a host gave. Components that set a size, a space or a shape go on any node with a `Role`; where a unit is needed it is the skin's own (a step of `GooeySkin::spacing`, a multiple of the line weight `GooeySkin::border`, a step of `GooeySkin::radii`). Pointer defaults are documented on each constructor (a mark or a gap is `Pickable::IGNORE` like `screen()`).

## Items
1. **A scroll viewport that is its own region: `scroll_viewport(gap, gutter)`.** The editor's `scroll_list` has 14 callers that insert markers and spawn children on the viewport entity it returns and read `ScrollPosition` on that same entity; `scroll_region` returns a region whose first child is the viewport, so every caller and reader would have to move together. `scroll_viewport` is **one bundle that is the scrolling viewport** (`Role::ScrollViewport`, `ScrollArea`, `ScrollGutter`, `LayoutGap`, `ScrollRail`): a flex column that fills its parent's room and may be nothing tall, scrolls vertically, keeps the host's gutter clear on its right and stands its children `gap` skin gaps apart; the host's markers and children are on it and `ScrollPosition` and `ComputedNode` are read on it.
   **How a viewport can be its own region: the rail is a sibling gooey spawns itself.** The rail needs a parent, and the viewport's `Children` must stay the host's. `manage_scroll_rails` (Style) gives every viewport that carries `ScrollRail` a rail (gooey's `list_rail`, `Role::ListRail`, a Bevy `Scrollbar` on the viewport) **as a child of the viewport's parent**, after it, marked `ScrollRailOf(viewport)`, once the viewport has a parent; it follows the viewport when it is re-parented and goes when `ScrollRail` is taken off or the viewport is despawned. `place_scroll_rails` puts it over the viewport's right edge, top aligned and as tall as it, from last frame's layout (the way the edge-fade overlays, also siblings, are placed), writing only when the place changed; `update_lists` shows it only while the content does not fit. So `Query<&Children>` of the viewport sees only the host's children; the *parent's* children include the rail (and the fade overlays), which the docs say. A viewport with no `ScrollRail`, a `scroll_region` and a `list` have no second rail: the new systems read only `ScrollRail` viewports.
   **Behaviour is the region's.** `ScrollGutter` marks it for `scroll_to_focus` (focus followed into view; for this role the viewport itself is the rows' column, so the row tops and the gap are read from it and not from a first child), `page_lists` (PageUp/PageDown) and `scroll_region_ends` (Home/End), all under the shared `keeps_nav_keys` predicate: a text area inside keeps its keys. Tests, real input: `the_viewport_entity_carries_the_hosts_marker_and_children_and_scrolls_them`, `the_wheel_and_keyboard_focus_move_the_scroll_position_on_the_viewport` (a wheel notch over it, then fourteen Tab presses: the focused row in view, `ScrollPosition` further), `the_rail_shows_only_while_the_content_overflows_and_is_not_the_hosts_child`, `the_rail_goes_with_the_viewport_or_its_marker` (flip after spawn), `the_rail_follows_a_viewport_that_changes_parent`, `a_hidden_viewport_shows_no_rail_and_no_stop`, `page_home_and_end_work_on_the_viewport_and_a_text_field_inside_keeps_its_keys`, `a_text_area_on_the_viewport_keeps_its_page_home_and_end`, `the_gutter_and_the_gap_are_the_hosts_and_a_change_restyles`, `a_scroll_region_and_a_list_are_unchanged_and_get_no_second_rail`. A new `Role` (`ScrollViewport`).
   **Caveats the docs state:** the children are flex items of a column, so a row that must keep its height carries `flex_shrink: 0` (a `scroll_region`'s items column does it for them); the viewport's parent must be a node the host controls and bounded in height.
